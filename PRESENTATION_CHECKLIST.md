## 🎤 **Q&A Preparation**

### **Common Questions & Answers**

#### **Q: How does the AI understand relationship dynamics?**
**A:** "The AI uses comprehensive user profiles including relationship goals, challenges, communication styles, and love languages. It applies evidence-based frameworks like Gottman Method and NVC to provide personalized therapeutic responses."

#### **Q: How do you ensure privacy between partners?**
**A:** "Each partner has completely separate chat sessions. The AI only analyzes both perspectives together when generating shared insights, and all data is encrypted and stored securely."

#### **Q: What makes this different from regular chatbots?**
**A:** "This isn't just a chatbot - it's a therapeutic AI that uses relationship psychology frameworks, provides crisis intervention, and generates actionable insights based on both partners' perspectives."

#### **Q: How scalable is this solution?**
**A:** "Built on cloud infrastructure with MongoDB Atlas, it can handle thousands of concurrent users. The modular architecture allows for easy scaling and feature additions."

#### **Q: What's the business model?**
**A:** "Currently free to demonstrate the technology. Future monetization could include premium features, therapist integration, or enterprise solutions for counseling centers."

#### **Q: How do you handle data security?**
**A:** "Enterprise-grade security with JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, data encryption, and comprehensive privacy protection. All data is stored securely in MongoDB Atlas with network access controls."
